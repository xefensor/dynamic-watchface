0.14.0 clearer settings — local development candidate

Settings now has section shortcuts for Appearance, Content & priority and Connection & help. The shortcuts work with touch and keyboard without changing browser history, and Save stays within reach.

Use the activity list to show or hide Music and Notifications. Notification details have three privacy choices after enabling the activity; hiding it keeps its previous choice. The activity-plugin switch leaves Music and Notifications independent and explains disabled plugin rows without changing your saved order or visibility.

Obsolete clock-mark copy and repeated setup/status text are removed. The title-only preview is named clearly, controls have larger touch targets, and all 17 languages remain covered.

The native watchface renderer, activity protocols and library pins are unchanged from 0.13.0. Twenty fresh native previews and real Clay browser interactions are checked separately from real-phone and physical-watch behavior. Earlier archives remain unchanged. This candidate is prepared locally with no public store submission; the plugin-preview hold remains.
