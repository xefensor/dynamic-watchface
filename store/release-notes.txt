0.13.0 shared Notifications activities — local development candidate

Notifications now uses the same primary and one-row secondary layouts as Timer, Music and compatible activity plugins. Source app identity and images remain visible, with optional message text in the standard detail area. App-only privacy avoids duplicate app headings. Both roles can open the Notifications app through Open Activity, including summaries without a body.

Reorder or show/hide Notifications in Dynamic’s existing Activity priority list. It remains Off by default. Its initial position is first when no Notifications position has been saved; explicit user order wins. The visibility row and privacy controls stay synchronized, retaining app-only privacy when re-enabled.

Every notification privacy mode shares one eight-second event window, capped by source expiry. Baselines do not replay history; late updates do not extend the event. Privacy, disconnection and expiry clear it. Optional additional vibration keeps its filter, Quiet Time and duplicate protections.

Activities 0.4.1 is pinned; Core remains 0.5.0. Native, portable, SDK, package and browser checks are recorded separately from real-phone or physical-watch testing. Earlier artifacts remain unchanged. This candidate is prepared locally, with no public store submission; the plugin-preview hold remains.
