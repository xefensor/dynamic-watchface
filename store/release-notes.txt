0.6.1 publication candidate

Fixes notification privacy when a source fully redacts or filters an active or queued message. Old private text now clears immediately.

Improves keyboard access to colour pickers, restores saved appearance after watch resets, and adds an independent daytime/Quiet Time colour preview. Clarifies that watchface banners and vibration are additional to PebbleOS alerts.

Keeps warnings visible on custom palettes, supplies a fallback for invisible notification icons, and pauses song-title paging beneath system windows. Includes full license notices in the standalone PBW, plus privacy, support and store materials.

The unified layout and existing preferences remain. Music and notifications require the Android plugin preview. Public store submission remains on hold while Pebble restricts plugin-based releases.
