0.7.0 plugin activities prerelease

Automatically discovers compatible activity plugins and presents timers or formatted readings in the shared main/footer area. A second activity or music summary appears only when it fits.

Adds a Plugin activities switch and provider status in all 17 languages. Uses separate Pebble Activities 0.1.0 and lean Watchface Core 0.5.0, preserving existing notification privacy and app settings.

Keeps the UUID, two readings, 24px music title, 20px artists and fixed footer. Identical font metrics are stored once to reduce the native image without changing the rendered fonts.

The reference timer is a separate native watchapp/provider. Last-reported countdowns do not certify its alarm or offline state. Stock PebbleOS only; native Workout session access and the future dashboard are not included. This is a sideloaded plugin preview; appstore submission remains held.
