0.11.0 simpler activities and full-width content — GitHub development prerelease

Plugins now publish content, optional detail and an image through the simple Activities 0.3.0 API. They do not select normal/compact layouts, arrange multiple labels or build meters. Earlier provider schemas remain compatible.

Dynamic's normal activity has a small icon/app header and full-width content below it. Song titles keep readable 24px type. Useful detail follows without Track/Artist boilerplate; missing optional artists stay empty. Clocks keep complete values and units. Compact activities remain separate and identifiable, and any activity can occupy either role.

Dynamic keeps your existing activity priorities and show/hide choices. Source privacy, expiry, observation age and native Timer alarms retain their ownership. The production Music adapter is used in native validation, including the reported long-title and missing-artist case.

Core 0.5.0 and Activities 0.3.0 are pinned. Earlier published artifacts and tags remain unchanged. Portable, SDK and emulator evidence is separate from physical-watch/real-phone testing. Pebble appstore submission remains held.
