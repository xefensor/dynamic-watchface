0.10.0 identified activities — GitHub development prerelease

Every activity has an app name, icon and current content. The primary is prominent; the optional secondary sits in its own divided band. Timer uses an hourglass and one countdown. Any compatible activity can occupy either role.

Reorder or show/hide activity apps in Dynamic’s Content settings. Music follows the same order as plugins. Compatible installed apps appear while idle; unavailable apps keep their saved position. Preferences stay in Dynamic, and other apps can choose their own order.

Meaningful metric labels, complete units, independent supporting items and explicit original-observation age remain intact. Essential primary content takes precedence over the optional secondary band. Private music stays transient and clears on disconnection or opt-out.

Core 0.5.0 and Activities 0.2.2 are pinned independently. Existing Timer 0.5.0 works with this watchface. Earlier published artifacts and tags remain unchanged. Portable, SDK and native-emulator checks are documented separately from real-phone or physical-watch checks. Pebble appstore submission remains held.
