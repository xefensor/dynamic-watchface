Dynamic keeps the time and your two Pebble readings visible while making room for compatible activities, music and notifications.

The large rounded clock settles into a compact header when an activity is showing. Primary content groups at the bottom above its context footer, leaving spare space above shorter content. An optional secondary activity occupies exactly one static row above it, with a small identifying symbol when available. Timer uses an hourglass and a countdown. Music has its own title, artwork and artist composition.

Compatible activity plugins publish standard or media summaries. Content can contain text, values or independent clocks; plugins supply meaning and Dynamic chooses the layout. Any activity can occupy either role. The primary expands when there is no secondary. Text starts at its beginning, truncates to the available space and never scrolls or automatically pages.

Reorder or show/hide activity apps in Dynamic’s settings. Music and Notifications follow the same order as compatible plugins and can take either role. Notifications starts first when no position has been saved; your explicit order wins. These choices belong to Dynamic, so other apps retain their own priorities.

Each item keeps its original expiry, and unavailable private content clears. Timer's public snapshot can predict its countdown only through its original validity; the timer watchapp owns state and alarms. A countdown reaching zero does not certify an alarm. Other saved sources that need a trust annotation are omitted. Dynamic displays activity information without plugin action controls.

The separate Open Activity helper can open an app associated with Dynamic's recently displayed primary or secondary activity. Dynamic retains only bounded destination metadata for this shortcut. The helper requires the phone connection and plugin support, and closes when the selected destination is unavailable. It can be launched from any watchface; it does not prove which face was immediately before it.

Choose your background, text and accent colours, share a colour code, or use a separate palette during the watch's Quiet Time. Follow Pebble's time format or choose 12-hour or 24-hour time. Settings support 17 languages and phone light/dark appearances. Section shortcuts reach Appearance, Content & priority and Connection & help. The activity list is the single place to show or hide Music and Notifications, and hiding Notifications preserves its privacy choice.

The optional Notifications activity can show only the app, an app/sender summary, or message text. It uses the same primary or one-row secondary layout in every privacy mode and defaults to Off. Its original eight-second window can be shortened by source expiry and does not extend on updates. Optional additional vibration also defaults to Off; these features do not replace PebbleOS notifications. Native Timeline Peek keeps priority over the face's content.

Dynamic targets Pebble Time 2. Live readings use Pebble's plugin system; Android is required for music and notification integration. The clock and date continue without the phone. Use Pebble's Music app for playback controls.
