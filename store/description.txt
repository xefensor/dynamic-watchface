Dynamic keeps the time and your two Pebble readings visible while making room for compatible activities, music and notifications.

The large rounded clock settles into a compact header when music is playing. Song titles stay readable at one fixed size, continuing through the remaining text once when needed. Artwork and artists keep a stable bottom position, with a simple fallback while a cover is missing.

Compatible activity plugins can supply two related pieces of information, such as remaining and elapsed time, or a reading and its estimated arrival time. Each can be text, a value or its own clock, with optional artwork and a meter based on real source data. Any activity can take the main or secondary place; extra content appears when it fits at a readable size.

Last-reported public content shows an uncertainty mark and its observation age. Each item keeps its own expiry, and unavailable private content clears. Native timers need a successful phone handoff; their originating watchapp remains responsible for pause/cancel state and the alarm. A countdown reaching zero does not certify an alarm. Dynamic displays activity information without app controls.

Choose your background, text and accent colours, share a colour code, or use a separate palette during the watch's Quiet Time. Follow Pebble's time format or choose 12-hour or 24-hour time. Settings support 17 languages and phone light/dark appearances.

Optional watchface notification banners can show only the app, an app/sender summary, or message text. They default to Off. Optional additional vibration also defaults to Off; these features do not replace PebbleOS notifications. Native Timeline Peek keeps priority over the face's content.

Dynamic targets Pebble Time 2. Live readings use Pebble's plugin system; Android is required for music and notification integration. The clock and date continue without the phone. Use Pebble's Music app for playback controls.

Version 0.9.0 is a GitHub sideload prerelease for the plugin preview. Public store submission is on hold while Pebble's preview restriction applies.
